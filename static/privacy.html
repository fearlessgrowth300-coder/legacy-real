Privacy Policy — Legacy Real

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Legacy Real is a private tool used by its owner to research real estate businesses and write outreach messages. It is not offered to the public.

Google account data

When the owner connects Gmail, the app requests only permission to send email (gmail.send) and to see the account's email address and name. It does not read, store, or delete any emails. The access token is stored only on the owner's private server and is used only to send messages the owner writes and approves. It is never shared, sold, or used for advertising.

Legacy Real's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other data

Business information collected is publicly available (websites, maps, public listings) and is kept on the owner's private server.

Removing access

Access can be revoked any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or with "Remove" in the app's Settings.
